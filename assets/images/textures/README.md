## Concrete prompt

Generate one full-frame orthographic photograph of natural poured architectural concrete, neutral light warm gray, realistic mineral variation and subtle weathering. Fine pores, irregular scattered pinholes, hairline scuffs and restrained shallow formwork impressions like a real cast concrete wall. Gentle uneven mottling across the entire surface, sparse thin horizontal and vertical formwork traces. Small scale surface details visible and tactile, but low enough contrast for dark website text over it. Flat diffuse daylight, uniform exposure, no vignette, no directional highlights. The whole image is only the material surface, no objects, words, typography, labels, logos, borders, perspective, dramatic cracks, or deep joints. Wide landscape approximately 1536 x 1024, high quality photograph rather than procedural noise. Aim for pale limestone-gray cement with almost no color saturation, not beige, blue or glossy. Tileable edges with consistent brightness.
