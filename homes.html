03 / KNOW WHAT YOU ARE BUDGETING FOR

The building, clearly defined.

Main-building scope

Our planning allowance is intended to cover labour, materials, routine site supervision and contractor overhead and margin for:

  • Normal foundations, ground floor, blockwork and required reinforcement
  • A simple engineered roof with sheet covering, rainwater goods and ceilings
  • Standard doors and windows; plaster, ceramic tiling and paint
  • Internal electrical wiring, basic fittings and plumbing
  • Bathroom fittings for the stated bathroom count
  • A basic kitchen sink, worktop and limited base storage

Materials, quantities and finish allowances are specified in your written quotation before you commit.

Budget separately

  • Land purchase, land registration and title/legal work
  • Design/professional fees, surveys, soil investigations and permit charges
  • Applicable taxes and statutory levies, itemised in the final quotation
  • Septic tank or treatment system, soakaway and external drainage
  • Water/electricity connections, borehole, storage tanks and pumps
  • Fence wall, gate, paving, landscaping and detached carport
  • Poor-ground treatment, retaining walls and major site clearance
  • Furniture, appliances, wardrobes, air conditioning, solar and premium upgrades

The advertised range is not a complete move-in budget. Ask us to price the external services and fees your particular site needs.

04 / BUILD AT AN AGREED PACE

Fund the work in stages.

A practical route for owners building from savings or arranging finance. We agree the full scope and a payment schedule before mobilisation; each stage has a budget, deliverables and inspection points.

01

Assess & agree

Review your land documents with the appropriate professionals, inspect the site, agree and pay for the design package, then develop the drawings and approve the BOQ.

02

Structure & roof

Fund the agreed foundation, walling and roofing stages. Receive progress records and inspection updates.

03

Services & finishes

Confirm fittings and finishes against the approved allowances. Agree any changes and their cost in writing.

04

Inspect & hand over

Review completed work, record defects for correction and receive your handover documentation.

Staged payments are payments for construction, not a KCE loan or a rent-to-own arrangement. The planned mobilisation payment is 30% of the agreed construction contract price, credited toward that price. The remaining 70% is paid against the milestones in your contract. Assessment, design, permits and other excluded costs are budgeted separately. Construction starts only after the required approvals and contract are in place; timing, pauses and any price-review terms are agreed in writing.

BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

Is land included?

No. The packages are for building on land you already own or arrange separately. Ownership, boundary, access and planning suitability must be checked before construction.

Can I build the two-storey designs at these prices?

No. The package budgets cover a single floor only. Two-storey homes, roof terraces, suspended slabs and future vertical extensions need their own engineered design and quotation.

Can I change the layout or add en-suite bathrooms?

Yes, we can discuss changes. More floor area, extra bathrooms, different roofing and upgraded finishes change the budget. The written scope and BOQ control what is included.

Do I pay for the full design?

Yes. Full design packages require payment of an agreed design fee. Request a quotation for the drawings and professional services your project needs; the scope, deliverables and fee are confirmed before design work begins. Payment is required before preparation and release of the full package. This fee is separate from construction payments. Browsing the reference gallery or making an enquiry is free.

How much do I pay to start?

Plan to pay 30% of the agreed construction price to mobilise. At the published budget ranges, this is GH₵89,400–108,000 for one bedroom, GH₵138,300–166,500 for two bedrooms, or GH₵178,800–214,500 for three bedrooms. This payment is part of the construction price, not an additional fee. Assessment, design, permits and other excluded costs are separate. Payment follows agreement on the site, required approvals, scope, BOQ and signed contract. Sending an enquiry does not commit you to a build.

Can you help if my house is already partly built?

Yes. Request a condition assessment and cost-to-complete review. The work already done must be checked; the new-build package prices cannot simply be reduced by a fixed percentage.
